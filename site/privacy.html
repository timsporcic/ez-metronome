Privacy Policy

EZ Metronome is a free and open source macOS app that does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

EZ Metronome does not collect personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, identifiers, location data, contacts, files, audio recordings, or any other user data.

Tracking and Advertising

EZ Metronome does not track users across apps or websites. It does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection tools.

Network Access

The app does not require an account and does not transmit app activity to a server. Metronome audio is generated locally on your Mac.

Mac App Store Privacy Label

For App Store privacy purposes, EZ Metronome should be listed as Data Not Collected.

Open Source

The source code is available at github.com/timsporcic/ez-metronome. The app is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the GitHub repository or contact the project maintainer through the repository.
